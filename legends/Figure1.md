# Reusable panel example group 1 | Hip fracture shifts the joint distribution of daily function

These A–H examples use the input contract described in INPUTS.md. The article's main figures use the complete production source in Reproducibility_Code.zip.

A, Case-standardized differences in the risk of at least two difficulties, with 95% participant-cluster intervals. B, Absolute risks; open symbols denote the reference and filled symbols fracture. C-E, Empirical case minus augmented reference probabilities for all 16 states. Columns encode dressing and eating; rows encode bed transfer and toileting; "both" denotes both activities on that axis. F, Cumulative severity risks from empirical case and simplex-projected reference distributions. G, Task-specific adjusted contrasts. H, Joint multiple-difficulty risk after 0.5% uniform smoothing compared with independent tasks under identical marginals. Circle, reference; square, fracture. Cohort colors are blue for HRS, orange for ELSA and green for CHARLS. All data axes measure 46 by 40 mm.

Source data: `Figure1_functional_burden.csv`. Panels A-B summarize the three cohorts; C, D and E show HRS, ELSA and CHARLS. Panel F preserves all four severity thresholds; G preserves the four individual tasks; H preserves both arms in all cohorts.
