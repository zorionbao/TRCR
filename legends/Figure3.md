# Reusable panel example group 3 | MOSAIC separates task prevalence from joint association

These A–H examples use the input contract described in INPUTS.md. The article's main figures use the complete production source in Reproducibility_Code.zip.

A-C, Exact five-player Shapley contributions to the case-standardized excess risk of at least two difficulties in HRS, ELSA and CHARLS. Points are estimates and intervals are the 2.5th-97.5th percentiles of 1,000 participant-level multiplier draws with simplex projection and 0.5% uniform smoothing. The four task players replace marginal risks; the fifth replaces all higher-order associations. D, Association-component multiplier distributions. E, All 32 hybrid distributions; coalition bits in increasing order encode dressing, eating, bed transfer, toileting and association. F, Sampling distributions under a known association-only shift; the vertical line is truth. F-G, Blue denotes MOSAIC joint and orange denotes independent tasks. G, RMSE in three 200-replicate benchmarks. H, Coverage of analytic component intervals; blue denotes marginal change, orange association change and green combined change. Each mechanism has 200 independent samples, with 1,200 reference and 400 case observations. All data axes measure 46 by 40 mm.

Source data: `Figure3_joint_composition.csv`. Panel D retains all 1,000 association draws per cohort. Panel E retains all 32 coalition values per cohort. Panel F retains every replicate from the association-only benchmark. Panels G-H retain all estimator-by-mechanism and component-by-mechanism summaries.
